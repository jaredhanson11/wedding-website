Our Story

how it all began

The Early Days

Jared and Mackensie met the modern way—on Hinge—while both were living in San Diego. Their first date was January 11th, 2024 at a beach front restaurant in Ocean Beach, just down the street from where Mackensie lived with her sister. There they were: ocean views, first-date nerves, and the ~slim~ possibility that this could be the start of their forever. According to Mackensie, the date became extra memorable when Jared got ICE'd by one of his friends who just happened to be sitting at the bar right next to them. Jared had skipped their weekly trivia night to go on this date… and his friend had no intention of letting that slide unnoticed.. Mackensie still insists this moment was the true test of character—and Jared passed.

Some of Our Adventures

After that, they were pretty much inseparable, talking all the time and making lots of plans. Their early days together consisted of trivia nights at PB Local, Batman marathons at Jared's place downtown, afternoons spent at the San Diego Zoo, and competitive game nights at Mackensie's place (shoutout to salad bowl). What Jared didn't realize at the time was that dating Mackensie meant signing up for a lifetime travel partner. Together they have taken twenty-five vacations, spanning the entire country. Their biggest adventure, though, was packing up their life in San Diego and moving across the country together to start a new chapter in the Big Apple.

The Proposal

New York was always meant to be temporary, a one year extended vacation. But Jared and Mackensie could've never imagined all they'd do in such a short amount of time. They traveled around the Northeast, dined at as many omakase spots as Mackensie could find, caught Yankees games, spent countless afternoons in Central Park, and hosted as many friends and family willing to make the trip to the concrete jungle. Six months in, Jared knew it was time. He knew he wanted to spend forever with someone that would move across the country with him, follow him as he learned the subway, and put up with him in a tiny New York apartment. His plan was simple. Take Mackensie on dates for a week straight - nice dinners, dressed up!, the works — just enough to keep her guessing. By Saturday morning, Mackensie had had enough. She wanted to sleep in, relax and not curl her hair for the millionth time that week. Jared had other plans. On that fateful September 27th, 2025, Jared brought her to Central Park one more time, the Manhattan skyline behind them, and got down on one knee.

And Now

And now, we're getting married! We can't wait to celebrate with all of our favorite people on

Questions & Answers

everything you need to know

The Details

Where is the wedding?

The ceremony and reception will both take place in San Diego, California. The full venue address and directions will be shared closer to the date.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 4:00 PM on December 11th. We kindly ask that all guests are seated by 3:45 PM. The welcome party on December 10th starts at 6:00 PM.

Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be outdoors. December evenings in San Diego can be cool, so we recommend bringing a light jacket or wrap.

Dress Code & Guests

What should I wear?

The welcome party on December 10th is casual. The ceremony and reception on December 11th are semi-formal. Think cocktail dresses and suits — no need for a full tux, but leave the jeans at home.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate guests who are specifically named on the invitation. If your invitation includes a plus one, it will be noted on your RSVP.

Are children welcome?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a great excuse for a night out!

Food & Drink

Will there be an open bar?

Yes! We'll have a full open bar during cocktail hour and the reception. Drink and be merry.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We want to make sure everyone is well-fed! Please note any dietary restrictions or allergies when you RSVP and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Logistics

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there is complimentary parking available on-site. Additional details will be provided as we get closer to the big day.

Are there hotel recommendations nearby?

We'll be sharing a hotel block with discounted rates for our guests soon. Stay tuned for details!

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask for an unplugged ceremony — please put your phones and cameras away while we exchange vows. Our photographer will capture every moment and we'll share the photos with everyone afterward. Snap away at the reception!

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by October 15th, 2026. This helps us finalize headcounts, seating, and catering. You can RSVP right on this website through the RSVP page.

Still have questions? Reach out to us directly — we're happy to help.
