Our Story

how it all began

The Early Days

Jared and Mackensie met the modern way —on Hinge— while both were living in San Diego. Their first date was January 11, 2024 at Wonderland Pub in Ocean Beach.

There they were: ocean views, first-date nerves, and the ~slim~ possibility this might be the start of their forever.

The evening became especially memorable when, as they arrived, Jared noticed his friend Ty sitting at the very same bar. Jared had skipped their weekly trivia night for the date, and Ty had no intention of letting that go unnoticed.

Halfway through the date, Ty got even by ICE'ing Jared. Jared obliged, and Mackensie still says it was the true test of character—and Jared passed.

Some of Our Adventures

After that, they were pretty much inseparable. Their early days together quickly settled into a rhythm: bingo nights at Harry's Taco Club, Batman marathons at Jared's downtown apartment, afternoons wandering the San Diego Zoo, and competitive game nights at Mackensie's place (shoutout to Salad Bowl).

What Jared didn't realize at the time was that dating Mackensie also meant signing up for a lifelong travel partner. Since that first date, the two of them have taken twenty-five trips together, exploring cities across the country.

Their biggest adventure, though, was packing up the life they had built in San Diego and moving across the country together to begin a new chapter in the Big Apple.

The Proposal

New York was always meant to be a one year extended vacation—so Jared and Mackensie got to work. They dined their way around the city, spent afternoons in Central Park, and hosted every friend and family member willing to make the trip to the concrete jungle.

Six months in, Jared just knew. He and Mackensie thrived in the move across the country, turned a tiny New York apartment into a (temporary) home, and now it was time to transition Mackensie from girlfriend to wife.

So early in the morning on September 27, 2025, Jared brought Mackensie to Central Park one more time. This time, with the Manhattan skyline behind them, Jared got down on one knee.

And Now

And now, they can't wait to celebrate their marriage in San Diego with all of their favorite people on

The Details

When should I RSVP?

We'll send out formal invitations soon with information on how to RSVP. In the meantime, please save the date on your calendar!

If you know ahead of time you cannot make it, we would appreciate you letting us know via text, email at mackensie.wedding@gmail.com, or here.

Where is the wedding?

The ceremony and reception will both take place at Ethereal Gardens in Escondido, California — about 45 minutes from San Diego International Airport with no traffic.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 3:30 PM on Friday, December 11th. We kindly ask that all guests are seated by 3:15 PM. Keep in mind that Friday afternoon traffic can be unpredictable — we recommend planning your transportation accordingly.

Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony and the reception will both be outdoors. The reception is under a temperature controlled tent, but December evenings in San Diego can be cool, so we recommend bringing a light jacket or wrap.

Dress Code & Guests

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate guests who are specifically named on the invitation. If your invitation includes a plus one, it will be noted on your RSVP.

Are children welcome?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a great excuse for a night out!

Food & Drink

Will there be an open bar?

Yes! We'll have a full open bar during cocktail hour and the reception. Drink and be merry.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We want to make sure everyone is well-fed! Please note any dietary restrictions or allergies when you RSVP and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Logistics

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there is complimentary valet parking available on-site. Additional details will be provided as we get closer to the big day.

Are there hotel recommendations nearby?

We'll be sharing a hotel block with discounted rates for our guests soon. Stay tuned for details!

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask for an unplugged ceremony — please put your phones and cameras away while we exchange vows. Our photographer will capture every moment and we'll share the photos with everyone afterward. Snap away at the reception!

Still have questions? We're happy to help.
Reach out to us directly via text, call, or email at mackensie.wedding@gmail.com.

Where to Stay

There's a wide range of places to stay around Temecula — here are a few we'd point you toward, from where we'll be to more budget-friendly and quieter options.

Pechanga Resort Casino

Temecula
Where we're staying

A lively resort, with a full casino, golf course, restaurants, and live music. We'll be staying here, so it's the easiest place to keep to continue celebrating after the wedding.

Tip: 10% off for AAA members, military, and emergency personnel.

Book the Stay & Save rate — 20% off

SpringHill Suites — Old Town

Old Town Temecula

A more affordable, modern option right in Old Town — walkable to restaurants and bars, and close to where the welcome party will be.

Temecula Creek Inn

Temecula · near Pechanga

A quieter alternative just minutes from Pechanga — a laid-back golf-resort setting if you'd rather wind down at the end of the night.

Heads up: wherever you decide to stay, budget in extra drive time — northbound traffic is at its worst in the afternoon and evening.

Things to Do

A few of our favorite ways to spend a day around San Diego — plus something close to the venue if you'd rather stay near wine country.

San Diego Zoo & Safari Park

Balboa Park & Escondido

The world-famous zoo in Balboa Park, plus its Safari Park up in Escondido — a short drive from the venue. A favorite of ours; we were season pass holders for years.

Tip: 10% off single-day passes with AAA.

Sunset Cliffs Natural Park

Point Loma

A picturesque walk along the bluffs at the edge of the Pacific — best at golden hour, and not too far from our old stomping grounds.

Golf

All over San Diego

San Diego is packed with great courses. The crown jewel is Torrey Pines, perched right on the coast.

Tip: Torrey Pines tee times go fast — book about 3 months ahead.

Beaches

Pacific Beach

Miles of coastline to choose from — our pick is Pacific Beach, with a lively boardwalk and plenty of spots to eat and drink right on the sand.

Annie's Canyon Trail

Solana Beach

A short but moderate 0.75-mile hike through naturally carved slot canyons in the San Elijo Lagoon Ecological Reserve.

Temecula Wine Country

Near the venue

Right by the welcome party and a short drive from the venue — dozens of wineries with tasting rooms and patios looking out over the vines. Easy to make a relaxed morning or afternoon of it.

Tip: Many tasting rooms take reservations on weekends — book ahead.
